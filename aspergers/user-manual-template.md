# User Manual

A one-page guide to working with, living with, and being friends with you — written by you and handed to the people who matter. It turns your needs from things others have to guess into things they can read.

Part of the [Asperger's](overview.md) section.

---

## How to Use It

- **Fill in each section** below. Keep the finished version to one page — people read one page.
- **Make two versions:** one for work (shorter, practical) and one for the people closest to you (fuller).
- **Draw from [Your Profile](overview.md#your-profile)** and your [Sensory audit](sensory.md#the-eight-sense-audit).
- **Update it once a year,** as part of the [Annual Review](../routines/annual-review.md).
- **You decide what goes in.** It can explain your needs without naming a diagnosis.

The italic lines are examples. Replace them with your own.

## Template

### How I Communicate
- *I take things literally. If you want something, say it directly — I won't be offended.*
- *Written messages are best for anything important. I'll answer better than I would on the spot.*
- *When I go quiet, I'm usually thinking or recharging — not upset with you.*
- *If I'm blunt, I'm being honest, not unkind. Tell me if something landed badly.*

### What Drains Me
- *Open-plan noise and fluorescent light*
- *Last-minute changes to plans*
- *Long group meetings without an agenda*

### What Helps
- *An agenda before meetings*
- *A heads-up when plans change, even small ones*
- *Time on my own after big social days*

### When I'm Overloaded
- **Signs:** *I go quiet, my answers get short, I need to leave.*
- **What helps:** *Space, fewer words, letting me step out. I'll come back.*
- **What doesn't:** *Asking me to explain right then, touching me without asking.*

### Feedback
- *Direct and specific works best: "This paragraph is unclear" beats "Maybe have another look?"*
- *Written feedback gives me time to process it properly.*

### Things That Might Be Misread
- *Not making eye contact means I'm listening, not ignoring you.*
- *Leaving an event early means I've hit my limit, not that I didn't enjoy it.*
- *Correcting a detail means I care about getting it right, not that I'm trying to win.*

### What I'm Great At
- *Spotting errors and gaps others miss*
- *Going deep on hard problems*
- *Following through on what I commit to*

### What I'd Love Help With
- *Telling me the unwritten rules here*
- *A signal when I've been talking about one topic too long*

## The Five-Line Card

For a new manager, a team, or anyone who won't read a full page. Five lines, nothing more:

1. **How to reach me:** *Written is best — email or chat.*
2. **How I communicate:** *Direct and literal. Please be direct with me.*
3. **What helps me work well:** *Clear priorities, written specs, a quiet space.*
4. **What can be misread:** *Quiet or blunt means focused, not upset.*
5. **What I'm best at:** *Finding the error, going deep, following through.*

For the people closest to you, pair the full manual with [For the People Close to You](for-people-close-to-you.md).
