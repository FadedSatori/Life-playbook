# Relationships

## Principles

1. **Show up consistently, not just when it's convenient.** Relationships are built in the unremarkable ordinary moments.
2. **Listen to understand, not to respond.** Most people are waiting to talk. Be the exception.
3. **Say the hard thing early.** Small resentments compound. Address things when they're small.
4. **Be interested, not interesting.** Ask questions. Remember the answers.
5. **Keep your word, or renegotiate it.** Broken commitments erode trust faster than most people realize.

## How to Show Up

- Be present when you're with someone — phone away, attention on them
- Celebrate others' wins without making it about you
- Reach out without needing a reason
- Remember what matters to the people you care about

## Conflict

- Separate the person from the problem
- Assume good intent until proven otherwise
- State your perspective clearly without attacking
- Repair quickly after a rupture — pride is expensive

## Who to Invest In

Energy is finite. Invest it in people who:
- Bring out a better version of you
- Are honest with you (including when it's uncomfortable)
- Show up for others, not just when they need something

Let relationships that drain consistently, without reciprocity, naturally reduce.
