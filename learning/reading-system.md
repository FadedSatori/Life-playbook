# Reading System

## Principles

1. **Read to change behavior, not to finish books.** A book that changes one thing is worth more than ten books noted and forgotten.
2. **It's okay to quit a book.** Sunk cost fallacy applies to reading. If it's not delivering, stop.
3. **Take notes in your own words.** Rephrasing forces understanding. Highlighting alone is passive.

## The System

**While reading:**
- Highlight sparingly — only what you'd actually act on or quote
- Write a one-line note in the margin when something connects to your life
- Fold the page corner (or use a bookmark note) when a section is worth revisiting

**After finishing:**
- Write 3–5 takeaways in your own words within 48 hours
- Identify one specific action or change to implement
- File notes in the relevant area of this playbook if they upgrade existing thinking

## What to Read

- Primary: books that build durable mental models (not news, not trending takes)
- Mix: ~60% nonfiction, ~40% fiction (fiction builds empathy and imagination)
- Bias toward classics and books >5 years old — the filter of time is real

## Retention

- Re-read great books. The second read after living more life is a different experience.
- Discuss books with someone. Explaining it forces gaps to surface.
- Return to your notes before starting a new book in the same subject area.
