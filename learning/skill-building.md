# Skill Building

## Principles

1. **Skills compound. Most don't start investing early enough.**
2. **The first 20 hours is the hardest.** Push through the frustration phase — competence feels like incompetence right before a breakthrough.
3. **Deliberate practice beats accumulated experience.** Time on task is less important than focused practice at the edge of your ability.
4. **One skill at a time.** Split focus produces mediocre results in multiple things. Depth first.

## How to Learn a New Skill

1. **Define the target.** What does "good enough" look like? Most skills don't require mastery — they require functional competence.
2. **Deconstruct it.** Break the skill into sub-skills. Find the 20% that delivers 80% of the capability.
3. **Find the fastest feedback loop.** The shorter the gap between attempt and correction, the faster you learn.
4. **Practice at the edge.** If it feels comfortable, you're not growing. If it feels impossible, back off slightly.
5. **Teach it.** Explaining forces you to confront what you don't actually understand.

## Meta-Skills Worth Investing In

These multiply the value of every other skill:
- Writing clearly
- Speaking well under pressure
- Managing your attention deliberately
- Learning how to learn

## When to Stop

A skill is worth abandoning when:
- The ceiling is lower than the cost to reach it
- A different skill would unlock more for the same effort
- You've been at plateau for months with no change in approach
