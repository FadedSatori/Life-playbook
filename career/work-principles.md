# Work Principles

## How I Work

1. **Deep work is the main event.** Protect large uninterrupted blocks. Meetings, messages, and admin fill the gaps — not the other way around.
2. **Do the hard thing first.** The most important task gets the first hours of the day, before the world's noise accumulates.
3. **Fewer commitments, done better.** Saying no is a skill. Guard your yes jealously.
4. **Finish what you start.** A bias toward completion over starting new things.
5. **Produce, don't consume.** Default mode is creation; consumption serves it.

## Quality Standard

- Good enough to ship beats perfect and delayed.
- But: don't ship something you're not willing to put your name on.
- Rework is expensive. Think clearly before starting, not while panicking near the deadline.

## Communication

- Respond to messages in batches, not continuously.
- Default to async; use real-time only when async doesn't fit.
- Be direct. Say what you mean clearly, without burying the point.
- Disagree openly, then commit to the decision.

## Career Principles

- Build skills that compound. Rare and valuable > common and safe.
- Reputation is built slowly and lost quickly. Act accordingly.
- Do work that would matter even if no one was watching.
- Learn from people who are where you want to be, not just peers.
