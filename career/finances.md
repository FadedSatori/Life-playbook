# Finances

## Rules

1. **Pay yourself first.** Savings are automated before lifestyle spending is possible.
2. **Live below your means, not at them.** The gap between income and spending is the source of all financial freedom.
3. **Time in market beats timing the market.** Invest consistently; don't try to be clever.
4. **Debt is a tool, not a lifestyle.** Only use leverage for assets that appreciate or generate income.
5. **Know your numbers.** Review spending monthly. What gets measured gets managed.

## Framework

**Income allocation (adjust to situation):**
- 50% — needs (housing, food, utilities, transport)
- 20% — savings & investments
- 20% — wants (guilt-free)
- 10% — giving / buffer

## Investing Defaults

- Index funds over stock-picking
- Max tax-advantaged accounts first (401k, IRA, HSA)
- Emergency fund: 3–6 months of expenses in cash, non-negotiable
- Automate everything that can be automated

## Mindset

- Wealth is built in years, not months. Patience is the edge.
- Lifestyle inflation is the enemy of financial freedom.
- Money buys time and options. That's what it's for.
- Never make a major financial decision under emotional pressure.
