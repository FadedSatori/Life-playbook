# Maxims

Compressed truth. One line each. Memorize the ones that hit.

---

## On Character & Identity

- What you do when no one is watching is who you are.
- Reputation is what people think you are. Character is what you are when it costs you something.
- Amateurs train until they get it right. Professionals train until they can't get it wrong.
- You are not your thoughts. You are what you do with them.
- The man who stands for nothing will fall for anything.
- Identity is upstream of behavior. Change who you are, not just what you do.
- How you do anything is how you do everything.
- Don't tell me your values. Show me your calendar and your bank account.
- The gap between who you are and who you present yourself to be is the source of most anxiety.
- A good name, once lost, takes a decade to rebuild — if it can be rebuilt at all.
- You become what you repeatedly do. Not what you intend to do. Not what you want to do.
- Your habits are your autobiography, written in real time.
- The person you are at your worst is more revealing than the person you are at your best.
- Dignity is not given. It is demonstrated consistently until it cannot be denied.
- Never let your public character exceed your private character. The gap will show eventually.
- A man is defined more by what he refuses than by what he pursues.
- The test of character is behavior under cost, not under comfort.
- What you tolerate in yourself, you invite into your life.
- Consistency is not a virtue. It is the container in which all virtues become real.
- Your default reaction under pressure is a report on who you've been training to be.

## On Action & Inertia

- Motion beats meditation. Start before you're ready.
- The cost of inaction compounds silently.
- Done is better than perfect. Shipped beats flawless.
- Preparation is permission. Do the work before you need it.
- Urgency without direction is just noise. Slow is smooth. Smooth is fast.
- The obstacle is the path — there's no way around, only through.
- A small action today is worth a thousand intentions tomorrow.
- Don't wish it were easier. Get better.
- Momentum is easier to maintain than to rebuild. Don't stop.
- The person who acts on incomplete information beats the person waiting for complete information.
- Hesitation is a decision. It just isn't one you made consciously.
- Every day you don't act on the thing you know you should do, you pay interest.
- Perfectionism is procrastination in a lab coat.
- The first step is almost always smaller than the paralysis makes it look.
- Most failures of execution are failures of starting, not failures of capability.
- Inaction is not neutral. It is a choice with consequences.
- The hard thing gets no easier with time. It only accumulates interest.
- If you're waiting for permission, you've already lost a round.
- Bias to action is a learnable skill. Practice it deliberately.
- Execution is the great separator. Ideas are cheap and universal. Execution is rare.

## On War & Competition

- Know your enemy, know yourself, and you need not fear a hundred battles.
- The best defense is not being where the attack lands.
- Win before the fight starts. Positioning is everything.
- Strength respects strength. Never negotiate from weakness if you can help it.
- Attack the plan, not the person. Destroy arguments, not people.
- Never interrupt an enemy while they're making a mistake.
- Winning ugly beats losing beautifully every time.
- If you find yourself in a fair fight, your tactics need work.
- The most dangerous opponent is the one you've underestimated.
- Don't fight the battle being offered. Fight the battle that serves your objective.
- Outprepare rather than outperform. The outcome is decided before the contest.
- Competitive advantage is almost never doing something others can't. It's doing what others won't.
- The professional treats competition as information. The amateur treats it as threat.
- Control the pace. The opponent who controls tempo controls the outcome.
- Never telegraph your intent. Surprise is force multiplied.
- The strongest position is one where winning requires the opponent to defeat themselves.
- In long games, integrity is a competitive advantage. Most people discount it. Don't.

## On Time & Mortality

- Memento mori: you will die. Act like it.
- The clock is always running. Comfort is the thief of time.
- Most people overestimate one year and underestimate ten.
- Time is the one resource you can't get back. Spend it accordingly.
- Urgency is a feeling. Importance is a fact. Don't confuse them.
- The present moment is the only place you actually live.
- Legacy is built in ordinary days, not extraordinary ones.
- You cannot manage time. You can only choose what you do with it.
- The years become what the days were.
- Seneca: it is not that we have little time; it is that we waste much of it.
- Every yes is a no to something else. Make the trade consciously.
- Deathbed clarity is available now. Use it.
- Procrastination is paying interest on a debt you haven't incurred yet.
- The future is built in five-minute increments. Guard them.
- An hour of your peak attention is worth ten hours of your distracted effort.
- Don't mourn the time you wasted. Use the clarity to protect what remains.
- Seniority does not make you immune to running out of time. It makes you closer to it.

## On Mind & Discipline

- The mind is a weapon. Train it or it trains you.
- Discipline is choosing between what you want now and what you want most.
- Clarity is power. A confused mind always says no.
- Pain is temporary. Quitting is permanent.
- You can't think your way into a new way of acting. Act your way in.
- Comfort is the enemy of growth. Seek the hard thing.
- The gap between stimulus and response is where freedom lives.
- Emotion is information, not instruction.
- The untrained mind is not calm — it is merely unexamined.
- What you think about between tasks is who you are becoming.
- Focus is not the absence of distraction. It is the repeated return to what matters.
- Suffering that produces insight is not wasted.
- The mind that cannot be uncomfortable cannot be trusted under pressure.
- Thoughts that recur without resolution are demanding action. Give them action or consciously dismiss them.
- The quality of your decisions is determined by the quality of your thinking at rested baseline, not at stressed peak.
- Rigorous honesty about your own state — energy, bias, motivation — is a cognitive superpower.
- Anxiety is usually the future dressed up as the present. It responds to action better than to analysis.
- Willpower depletes. Systems do not. Build the system.
- A journal is not therapy. It is a forcing function for precision in thinking.
- The person who can be alone with their thoughts without escape is among the most formidable people alive.

## On Relationships & People

- Show up. The appearance is 80% of love and 80% of leadership.
- People remember how you made them feel long after they forget what you said.
- Trust is built in drops and lost in buckets.
- The quality of your life is largely the quality of your relationships. Everything else is footnote.
- You cannot change people. You can only change who you spend time with.
- The person who cannot be a good friend cannot be a good anything else.
- Generosity compounds. Stinginess also compounds.
- Listening is not waiting to speak. It is the act of letting another person be known.
- The most powerful thing you can say to a struggling person is: I believe in you.
- Invest in people who invest back. Asymmetric relationships drain everything.
- Mean what you say at funerals while the person can still hear you.
- The people who push you hardest often care most. Comfort is not always kindness.
- Great relationships are not found. They are built, maintained, and sometimes fought for.
- Be easy to reach in a crisis. It is the standard by which people remember you.
- The friend who tells you the hard truth is worth more than ten who protect your feelings.
- Judge slowly. Relationships that start too hot cool fast. Patterns measured in years tell the truth.
- Loyalty given and not returned is a lesson. Learn it once.
- Don't confuse being liked with being trusted. They are different things earned by different behavior.
- Your network is not a collection of contacts. It is a record of value you've provided.
- Loneliness is corrected by depth, not by volume. One honest relationship is worth forty casual ones.

## On Money & Wealth

- Spend less than you earn. Everything else is commentary.
- Wealth is not income. It is the gap between what you earn and what you spend, compounded.
- Financial freedom is bought one saved percentage point at a time.
- Never borrow money to buy depreciating assets.
- The first fifty thousand is the hardest. Compounding does more work as it scales.
- Lifestyle inflation is how people earn more and own less.
- Most financial disasters are not caused by bad luck. They are caused by leverage meeting a surprise.
- Risk is not volatility. Risk is permanent loss of capital.
- Diversification is protection against ignorance. Concentrate when you have genuine edge.
- The best investment is in a skill with durable demand that no one else has.
- Most investment decisions made in states of excitement or fear are wrong.
- Understand every financial instrument you own well enough to explain it to a twelve-year-old.
- The person who can wait almost always beats the person who can't.
- Wealth creation is slow and boring until it is sudden and dramatic.
- Never let the urgent tax bill be a surprise. Know your numbers.
- A business that requires your constant presence is a job. Build systems or stay employed.
- Price is what you pay. Value is what you get. Know the difference before you transact.
- Debt is fine for assets that appreciate or produce income. It is toxic for everything else.
- The floor of your financial life is determined by your spending habits, not your earning capacity.
- Your relationship with money reflects your relationship with discipline. Improve one; the other follows.

## On Learning & Mastery

- The expert was once a beginner who refused to quit.
- Reading without application is entertainment.
- The person who has been uncomfortable more often has learned more.
- Learn from first principles, not just from received wisdom. Understand the why or you don't understand at all.
- Feedback is the food of improvement. Seek it aggressively.
- The feeling of confusion is the feeling of growth. Don't flee it.
- Teach what you learn immediately. The act of teaching exposes exactly what you don't understand.
- Mastery is not the absence of mistakes. It is the shortening of the interval between mistake and correction.
- The most important skill is learning how to learn. It multiplies everything else.
- Copy before you create. Every master began as an imitator. Don't skip that step.
- Quantity produces quality when you're learning. Finish things. Ship things. Accumulate reps.
- The plateau is not failure. It is the consolidation before the next breakthrough. Stay.
- Find the best person in the world at what you want to do. Study how they think, not just what they do.
- Twenty hours of deliberate practice outperforms two hundred hours of casual repetition.
- The best mentors give you harder problems, not easier ones.
- Reading builds the map. Doing builds the territory. You need both, but doing is senior.
- Embrace being a beginner. Beginner's mind is a permanent competitive advantage.
- The half-life of specific technical knowledge is short. The half-life of clear thinking is long. Invest accordingly.

## On Health & the Body

- Your body is the vehicle for everything else. Neglect it and the whole mission degrades.
- Strength is not vanity. It is the physical expression of discipline and a longer operating lifespan.
- Sleep is not rest. It is biological maintenance that cannot be deferred without debt.
- The body keeps score. Stress you ignore metabolically it does not.
- Consistency over intensity. The person who trains at 70% every day beats the person who trains at 100% twice a week.
- Recovery is not optional. It is half the training.
- Movement is medicine. Stillness is slow decay.
- What you eat is infrastructure, not entertainment.
- Inflammation is the common pathway of most chronic failure. Diet and sleep are the two biggest levers.
- You do not have a body. You are a body. Treat it accordingly.
- The physical standard you maintain in your thirties sets the baseline for your fifties.
- The hard workout you didn't want to do is often the one that produces the most growth.
- Pain is a signal. Suffering is optional. Know the difference.
- Most people's energy problems are sleep problems in disguise.
- The training log is data. Without it, you're training by feel, which is training without feedback.
- Hydration and sleep are the most underrated performance variables available at zero cost.
- Stress without recovery produces breakdown, not adaptation. Earn your rest.

## On Fear & Courage

- Courage is not the absence of fear. It is moving forward while fear is present.
- The thing you are most afraid to do is often the most important thing you could do.
- Most fears are not about the thing itself. They are about what the failure would mean about you.
- Avoidance amplifies. Every fear you run from gets bigger.
- The first step into the feared thing is the hardest. It gets easier from there, always.
- Cowardice has a higher long-term cost than failure.
- Fear is pointing at what matters. Don't ignore the signal.
- Do the hard thing first. Dread left unaddressed consumes the rest of the day.
- The brave person isn't immune to doubt. They've just learned that doubt is not a veto.
- Nothing shrinks faster than a fear you've decided to walk toward.
- Regret is the residue of cowardice. It accumulates for decades.
- Comfort zones are comfortable because nothing new is happening inside them.
- The moment before the feared action is the worst moment. The doing is almost always better.
- You can be scared and right. Fear of the unknown is not evidence of being wrong.
- Every time you act in spite of fear, you recalibrate what you're capable of.
- Courage is a muscle. It atrophies without use.
- The person who has done more things afraid has a bigger life than the person who waited until they weren't.
