# Quotes

Compressed philosophy from people who earned their perspective. Read slowly.

---

## On Discipline & The Inner Life

> *"You have power over your mind, not outside events. Realize this, and you will find strength."*
> — **Marcus Aurelius**

The Stoic core: control the controllable. Everything else is noise.

---

> *"The impediment to action advances action. What stands in the way becomes the way."*
> — **Marcus Aurelius**

Not comfort from adversity — instruction. The obstacle is the curriculum.

---

> *"Waste no more time arguing what a good man should be. Be one."*
> — **Marcus Aurelius**

Discussion is not action. The argument ends where the behavior begins.

---

> *"It's not that I'm so smart. It's that I stay with problems longer."*
> — **Albert Einstein**

Persistence masquerading as intelligence. The bottleneck is usually endurance, not brilliance.

---

> *"A ship is always safe at shore, but that is not what it's built for."*
> — **Albert Einstein**

Safety is a slow form of death for anyone built to move.

---

## On Action & Courage

> *"Do not pray for an easy life. Pray for the strength to endure a difficult one."*
> — **Bruce Lee**

Strength is built through resistance. Wishing for less resistance is wishing to stay weak.

---

> *"I fear not the man who has practiced 10,000 kicks once. I fear the man who has practiced one kick 10,000 times."*
> — **Bruce Lee**

Depth annihilates breadth in any real confrontation.

---

> *"Appear weak when you are strong, and strong when you are weak."*
> — **Sun Tzu**

Information asymmetry is a weapon. Never reveal your actual state.

---

> *"Victorious warriors win first and then go to war, while defeated warriors go to war first and then seek to win."*
> — **Sun Tzu**

Preparation is the strategy. The battle is just confirmation.

---

## On Identity & Potential

> *"There is no spoon."*
> — **Neo, The Matrix**

The constraints you believe in are the only constraints that bind you. Question the frame before accepting its limits.

---

> *"I know kung fu."*
> — **Neo, The Matrix**

When you commit to the download — to truly learning something — it changes you instantly and completely. The bottleneck is always commitment, not capacity.

---

> *"The only way to deal with an unfree world is to become so absolutely free that your very existence is an act of rebellion."*
> — **Albert Camus**

Freedom is a posture before it's a circumstance.

---

## On Endurance & Excellence

> *"I've missed more than 9,000 shots in my career. I've lost almost 300 games. 26 times I've been trusted to take the game-winning shot and missed. I've failed over and over and over again in my life. And that is why I succeed."*
> — **Michael Jordan**

Failure is not the opposite of success. It's the substrate it grows from.

---

> *"The cave you fear to enter holds the treasure you seek."*
> — **Joseph Campbell**

The resistance pointing at a thing is a signal, not a stop sign.

---

> *"Memento mori."*
> — **Stoic practice**

Remember that you will die. Not as a threat — as a clarifier. It strips pretense and reveals what actually matters.

---

## On Knowledge & Thinking

> *"The first principle is that you must not fool yourself — and you are the easiest person to fool."*
> — **Richard Feynman**

Self-deception is the primary cognitive failure mode. Interrogate your own beliefs first.

---

> *"The map is not the territory."*
> — **Alfred Korzybski**

Your model of reality is not reality. Act on the best available map while knowing it's wrong.

---

> *"The snake which cannot cast its skin has to die."*
> — **Friedrich Nietzsche**

Growth requires shedding old identity. Clinging to who you were is how you fail to become who you could be.

---

## On War, Pressure & Adversity

> *"If you're going through hell, keep going."*
> — **Winston Churchill**

The only correct response to the middle of something hard is forward motion.

---

> *"Hard times create strong men. Strong men create good times. Good times create weak men. And weak men create hard times."*
> — **G. Michael Hopf**

The cycle is real. Which part are you in? Which part are you creating?

---

> *"Smooth seas do not make skillful sailors."*
> — **African proverb**

Difficulty is not punishment. It's training.
