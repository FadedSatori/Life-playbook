# Core Principles

The foundation everything else is built on. When in doubt, return here.

## Values

1. **Integrity over comfort.** Do what you said you'd do, especially when it's inconvenient.
2. **Long game thinking.** Optimize for who you're becoming, not how you feel right now.
3. **Ownership.** No excuses, no blame. You are the author of your life.
4. **Depth over breadth.** A few things done with full commitment beat many things done halfway.
5. **Presence.** Where you are, be all there.

## Decision Filter

Before committing to anything significant, ask:
- Does this align with my values?
- Would I be proud of this in 10 years?
- Am I saying yes out of genuine desire, or out of fear/guilt/ego?

If two of three answers are no, decline.

## Non-negotiables

Things that are not up for debate regardless of circumstance:
- Sleep 7–8 hours
- Move every day
- Keep your word
- Protect deep work time
