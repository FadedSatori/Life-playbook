# Longevity Protocols

The goal: not just to live long, but to be physically and cognitively exceptional at 70, 80, 90. Healthspan over lifespan. The interventions with the most evidence, ranked by leverage.

*Built on work from Peter Attia (Outlive), Andrew Huberman, Rhonda Patrick, and David Sinclair.*

---

## The Four Horsemen — What Actually Kills You

95%+ of deaths in the developed world come from four diseases:
1. Cardiovascular disease
2. Cancer
3. Neurodegenerative disease (Alzheimer's, dementia)
4. Metabolic dysfunction (type 2 diabetes and its cascade)

Everything in this file targets the *upstream causes* of these four — years and decades before they manifest.

---

## The #1 Longevity Lever: Cardiorespiratory Fitness

**VO2 max** — the maximum rate at which your body can use oxygen during exercise — is the single strongest predictor of longevity across all-cause mortality studies. Moving from "low" to "above average" VO2 max reduces all-cause mortality by ~45%. Moving from "average" to "elite" reduces it further.

**How to build it:**
- **Zone 2 cardio:** 150–180 min/week of low-intensity steady-state (conversational pace, nose breathing). Builds mitochondrial density — the engine of every cell.
- **VO2 max intervals:** 1–2x/week. 4 min at max sustainable effort, 4 min recovery, 4 rounds. This raises the ceiling.

## Strength Training as Longevity Protocol

Muscle mass is one of the strongest predictors of longevity after 50. Sarcopenia (muscle loss with age) accelerates every disease process. The people who maintain muscle into old age live longer and live better.

**Targets for longevity (not aesthetics):**
- Grip strength (strongly correlated with cardiovascular health and all-cause mortality)
- Hip hinge strength (deadlift pattern) — protects the posterior chain, prevents falls
- Carry capacity (farmer's carry, loaded walks)
- Overhead pressing — shoulder health and functional overhead reach
- Single-leg stability — balance, fall prevention, knee and hip health

**Minimum effective dose:** 3 sessions/week, compound movements, progressive overload.

## Sleep as the Primary Recovery Protocol

Every longevity protocol fails without sleep. See [sleep.md](health/sleep.md) for the full protocol.

**Longevity-specific mechanisms:**
- **Glymphatic system activation** — during deep sleep, the brain flushes amyloid plaques (Alzheimer's precursors). Chronic sleep deprivation accelerates neurodegeneration.
- **Growth hormone release** — the majority of GH is released during slow-wave sleep. Critical for cellular repair.
- **Immune function** — one poor night reduces NK cell activity 70%. Chronically short sleep dramatically increases cancer risk.

7–9 hours is not optional. It is medicine.

## Metabolic Health — The Foundation

**Insulin sensitivity** is the metabolic master variable. Poor insulin sensitivity is upstream of cardiovascular disease, cancer, neurodegeneration, and obesity.

**What controls it:**
- Exercise (the most powerful lever — muscle is the primary glucose sink)
- Sleep (one week of 6-hour nights induces near-diabetic insulin resistance in healthy adults)
- Diet composition (minimize ultra-processed food, refined carbohydrates, vegetable oils)
- Time-restricted eating (8–10 hour eating window — the evidence for longer fasting is thinner)

**Metrics to track:**
- Fasting glucose (target: 70–90 mg/dL)
- HbA1c (target: below 5.4%)
- Triglycerides/HDL ratio (target: below 1.0)
- Waist circumference (best simple metabolic proxy)

## Hormesis — Stress That Strengthens

Brief, controlled exposure to stressors that activate the body's adaptive response. The biology of antifragility.

**Cold exposure:**
- Cold shower: 2–3 min, 3–4x/week. Activates brown fat, improves mood (via norepinephrine), mild metabolic benefit.
- Cold plunge: 11 min/week total (across sessions) at ~55°F is the evidence-based target for metabolic and mood benefit.
- Do it in the morning to increase alertness and dopamine.

**Heat exposure (sauna):**
- 4x/week, 20 min at 175°F+ → 40% reduction in cardiovascular mortality (Finnish cohort study, 20 years)
- Activates heat shock proteins (cellular cleanup), improves cardiovascular function, growth hormone response
- Post-workout sauna extends the growth hormone spike

**Fasting:**
- Time-restricted eating (16:8) — the evidence is solid for metabolic benefit with minimal downside
- Extended fasting (24–72 hours, 2–4x/year) — activates autophagy (cellular cleanup); meaningful for longevity but requires medical consideration

## Cognitive Longevity

The brain is not separate from the body. Every physical intervention above also protects the brain.

**Specific to neurological protection:**
- **Aerobic exercise** → the most evidence-backed intervention for Alzheimer's prevention
- **Sleep** → glymphatic clearance (see above)
- **Social connection** → loneliness is as damaging as smoking 15 cigarettes/day (Holt-Lunstad meta-analysis)
- **Cognitive challenge** → lifelong learning, novel skill acquisition, complex problem-solving delays neurodegeneration
- **Omega-3 supplementation** → DHA is the primary structural fat of the brain; 2g EPA/DHA/day is well-supported

## The Centenarian Decathlon

Peter Attia's framework: decide what physical tasks you want to be able to perform at 80, and train backward from that target now.

**Example targets at 80:**
- Carry 30 lbs up a flight of stairs without stopping
- Get off the floor from a seated position without using your hands
- Walk 1 mile in under 20 minutes
- Maintain balance on one leg for 10 seconds with eyes closed

Working backward: to perform these at 80, you need to be significantly *above* that level at 50 and 60. Decline is linear if you let it be. It is not if you don't.

**Start training for 80 now, regardless of current age.**
