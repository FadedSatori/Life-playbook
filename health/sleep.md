# Sleep

## Why It's the Priority

Sleep is the highest-leverage health intervention. It regulates every other system — metabolism, cognition, mood, recovery, immune function. Sacrifice it last, not first.

## Rules

1. **Consistent wake time, every day.** Anchor the circadian rhythm here; bedtime will follow.
2. **No screens 30 minutes before bed.** Non-negotiable on the hard nights when it matters most.
3. **Keep the bedroom cold (65–68°F / 18–20°C), dark, and quiet.**
4. **No caffeine after 1pm.** Half-life is ~6 hours; it's still in your system at midnight.
5. **No alcohol within 3 hours of sleep.** It fragments sleep architecture even if it helps you fall asleep.

## Wind-Down Protocol (30 min before bed)

1. Dim all lights
2. Write tomorrow's top 3 priorities (offload the mental queue)
3. Read fiction or something low-stakes
4. No news, no email, no social media

## If You Can't Sleep

- Get out of bed after 20 minutes of lying awake — don't associate bed with frustration
- Do something calm in dim light, return when sleepy
- Never clock-watch
