# Fitness

## Principles

1. **Consistency beats intensity.** Showing up at 70% every day outperforms heroic efforts followed by burnout.
2. **Strength is the foundation.** Build muscle first — it improves everything else (metabolism, posture, resilience, longevity).
3. **Movement is non-negotiable; the format is flexible.** If you can't do the full session, do 10 minutes. Never zero.

## Training Framework

- **Frequency:** 4 days lifting, 2 days cardio/mobility, 1 day rest
- **Lifting split:** Push / Pull / Legs / Full-body
- **Cardio:** Zone 2 (conversational pace) for longevity; one hard session per week for VO2 max
- **Minimum effective dose on bad weeks:** 3 lifts + 2 walks

## Rules

- Track lifts. If you're not tracking, you're not progressing.
- Leave one rep in the tank on most sets. Save max effort for peak weeks.
- Deload every 4–6 weeks (reduce volume 40%, keep intensity).
- Don't train fasted for strength work; do train fasted for low-intensity cardio if preferred.

## Recovery Signals

Back off if you notice two or more:
- Persistent joint pain (not muscle soreness)
- Sleep quality declining
- Resting heart rate elevated >5 bpm above baseline
- Motivation tanking for multiple consecutive days
