# Nutrition

## Principles

1. **Protein first.** Target 0.7–1g per pound of bodyweight. Everything else is secondary.
2. **Eat food, mostly real, not too much.** Minimize ultra-processed food — not because it's morally wrong, but because it works against satiety and energy.
3. **Don't outsource your defaults.** Know what you're eating most days; leave flexibility for everything else.
4. **Hunger is not an emergency.** Learn to distinguish true hunger from boredom, stress, or habit.

## Daily Defaults

- Breakfast: high protein, minimal sugar — sets the tone for the day
- Lunch: largest meal if possible (energy when you need it most)
- Dinner: lighter; digestion slows in the evening
- Snacks: only if genuinely hungry, not as a habit

## Rules

- Drink water first when you think you're hungry
- Don't buy food you don't want to eat — environment beats willpower
- Eat slowly enough to notice fullness (it lags ~15 min behind)
- Track food for 2 weeks per year to recalibrate intuition

## What to Limit (Not Eliminate)

- Ultra-processed snacks
- Liquid calories (alcohol, juice, fancy coffee drinks)
- Eating out of boredom or stress
