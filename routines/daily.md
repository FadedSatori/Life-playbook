# Daily Routine

## Morning (first 60–90 minutes)

The morning sets the context for the day. Protect it from reactive inputs.

1. **No phone for the first 30 minutes.** Start the day on your terms, not someone else's agenda.
2. **Move.** Even 10 minutes — walk, stretch, lift. Gets the body and mind into gear.
3. **Write today's top 3 priorities.** One sentence each. These are the only things that must get done.
4. **Start the most important task before checking messages.**

## Evening (last 30 minutes)

The evening determines tomorrow's baseline.

1. **Close open loops.** Write down anything unfinished or on your mind.
2. **Set tomorrow's top 3.** Tomorrow's you will thank today's you.
3. **Wind down.** Follow the [sleep wind-down protocol](../health/sleep.md).

## Daily Minimums

Non-negotiables that don't disappear regardless of how busy the day is:
- 7+ hours of sleep
- Some form of movement
- At least one meaningful conversation or connection
- One block of focused work on what matters most

## Keystone Habits

These create a pull effect on everything else:
- Morning movement → energy and mood improve → better decisions all day
- Writing down priorities → clarity → less time wasted on low-value work
- Consistent sleep time → recovery → cognitive function → everything gets easier
